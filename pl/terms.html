1. Driving Requirements

To drive a quad bike on our tours, the following rules apply:

  • You must hold a valid physical driver's license (Category B for passenger cars) valid for at least 1 year. Temporary or copy licenses are not accepted.
  • The physical driver's license must be presented at our shop prior to the tour start.
  • All drivers must be at least 18 years old.
  • Passengers must be at least 7 years old to ride on a double quad.

2. Safety & Code of Conduct

Your safety is our priority. Participants must strictly follow the guide's instructions at all times:

  • Wearing the provided helmet is mandatory throughout the tour.
  • Dangerous driving, drifting, overtaking other quads without authorization, or leaving the designated trails is strictly prohibited.
  • Zero-tolerance policy for driving under the influence of alcohol or drugs. Offending participants will be immediately excluded from the tour without refund.

We understand plans can change. Our refund policy is as follows:
