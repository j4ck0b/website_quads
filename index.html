Teide National Park Quad Expedition

Tenerife's #1 Volcanic Adventure

⭐ 4.9 (480+ Reviews)

Embark on the ultimate off-road adventure in Tenerife! This premium quad excursion takes you from the beautiful village of Vilaflor directly into the breathtaking landscapes of Mount Teide National Park. You will drive through majestic pine forests of Corona Forestal, cross amazing volcanic lava fields, and ascend to over 2,000 meters above sea level to admire the peak of Mount Teide from stunning panoramic viewpoints. Perfect for couples, families, and adventure seekers.

🗺️ Route Teide & Vilaflor
🚜 Quad Bikes Premium 500cc
🗣️ Languages PL, EN, ES
