Teide National Park Quad Expedition

Tenerife's #1 Volcanic Adventure

⭐ 4.9 (480+ Reviews)

Embark on the ultimate mountain adventure in Tenerife! This premium quad excursion takes you from our shop in Las Américas, riding along scenic mountain roads all the way to the breathtaking landscapes of Mount Teide National Park. You will drive through the majestic pine forests of Corona Forestal and cross amazing volcanic lava fields, ascending to over 2,000 meters above sea level. Along the way, we will make several stops to soak in the breathtaking views and enjoy photo sessions, ensuring you capture memories to last a lifetime as you admire the peak of Mount Teide from stunning panoramic viewpoints. Perfect for couples, families, and adventure seekers.
