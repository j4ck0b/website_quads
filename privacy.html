Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 5, 2026

At Prime Quads Tenerife (Extreme Prime Tours SL), we are committed to protecting and respecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or book our quad tours.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us when booking a tour, contacting us via our website, or communicating with us via WhatsApp. This may include:

  • Personal identification details (Name, Email address, Phone number).
  • Booking details (Preferred date, time, number of drivers/passengers).
  • Driver's license details (verification that you hold a valid Category B license for driving quads on public roads).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To process and manage your tour bookings and send reservation updates.
  • To coordinate logistics and ensure safety requirements (such as driver's license validation) are met.
  • To contact you directly via WhatsApp or email regarding your tour status.
  • To comply with local safety, legal, insurance, and tax regulations in Spain.

3. Data Sharing & Security

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share data only with trusted third-party service providers (such as insurance companies for your mandatory excursion coverage) under strict confidentiality agreements. We implement advanced technical and organizational security measures to prevent unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure of your personal data.

4. Your Rights (GDPR)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to access, rectify, or request the deletion of your personal data held by us. You can exercise these rights at any time by contacting us directly at our email address or business location.
