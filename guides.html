How to Plan Your Mount Teide Quad Tour (Ultimate Guide)

Mount Teide is Tenerife's crowning glory, ascending over 3,700 meters above the Atlantic Ocean. Exploring the high-altitude forests and volcanic lava fields on a powerful 550cc quad bike is one of the most popular activities on the island. To ensure you have a seamless and safe adventure, here is everything you need to know about planning your excursion.

1. Choose the Right Time of Day

Teide quad excursions typically run during three times of the day, each offering a completely unique visual atmosphere:

  • Morning Tours (09:30): Offer the highest visibility, perfect for photography and enjoying the crisp mountain air.
  • Afternoon Tours (13:00): Best for warm weather, especially as you ascend past 2,000 meters above sea level where the climate cools down significantly.
  • Sunset Tours (18:30): The ultimate romantic choice. Riding above the clouds while the sky turns orange and purple is an unforgettable experience.

2. What to Bring & Wear

Since you will be ascending from sea level to the high mountains, temperature drops of up to 10-15°C are common. Here is what is essential:

  • Driver's License: A physical Category B driver's license valid for at least 1 year is mandatory. Copies or digital versions are not accepted by traffic authorities.
  • Footwear: Fully closed-toe athletic shoes or hiking boots are mandatory. Strictly no sandals or flip-flops allowed.
  • Clothing: Wear comfortable layers. Even on hot summer days, the pine forests of Corona Forestal can be chilly. Prime Quads provides warm, windproof jackets for all participants free of charge.

3. Book in Advance

Because we focus on small-group excursions (maximum of 4 quads per tour guide) to maintain premium safety and dynamic group communication, spots fill up very quickly. We recommend booking at least 3 to 5 days in advance via our WhatsApp instant reservation widget.

Top Quad Biking Routes & Off-Road Trails in Tenerife

Tenerife's microclimates and volcanic topography offer some of the most diverse off-road riding terrain in Europe. From coastal sand tracks to pine forests and high-altitude lava deserts, here are the top trails that make quad excursions in Tenerife a must-do.

The Corona Forestal Pine Forest Trail

This classic route circles the base of Mount Teide National Park. Riding through the towering Canary Island pine trees offers a beautiful blend of dirt trails and sweeping asphalt corners. The scent of pine resin and the frequent sea-of-clouds phenomenon make this one of the most atmospheric forest drives on earth.

The Teide Volcanic Lava Fields

Ascending above 2,000 meters, you enter a surreal, lunar landscape shaped by centuries of volcanic eruptions. Riding through black lava tongues and obsidian fields with the massive peak of Mount Teide in front of you is a humbling experience. We make several stops at iconic viewpoints (such as Boca Tauce) for panoramic group photo sessions.

Why Choose a Guided Route?

Mount Teide National Park is a protected UNESCO World Heritage site. Off-road riding is strictly regulated to preserve the delicate endemic flora and fauna. By joining a professional tour with Prime Quads, you ride along authorized, legal off-road trails with expert guides, ensuring safety and environmental conservation while enjoying the absolute limit of what 550cc quad bikes can do.
